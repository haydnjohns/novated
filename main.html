How to read this: this ledger is a planning tool, not tax or financial advice. It uses simplified assumptions common to retail novated lease quotes: the ATO statutory formula method for FBT (based on the vehicle's drive-away price, held for a full year); 2026–27 Australian resident income tax brackets plus the Medicare levy (assuming a single person with no dependents, so the low-income levy phase-in and family thresholds may not match your exact situation) and the Low Income Tax Offset; a flat nominal interest rate applied evenly across each pay period; and no allowance for HECS/HELP repayments, the Medicare Levy Surcharge, or private health insurance rebates, all of which can also change with your taxable income.

Tax rules, FBT settings and the EV exemption are all subject to change — check the figures against a quote from your employer's salary packaging provider, or a registered tax agent, before you commit.
