How to read your quote (Sample Guide)

We've pre-filled the calculator below using a real SG Fleet quote for a 2026 ZEEKR 7X. If you are holding your own quote, here is where to find the numbers to overwrite ours:

  • Income & Pay Cycle: Look at your "Indicative quote" summary. Our sample assumes a $190,000 salary paid "Bi-monthly" (which means 24 pay cycles a year).
  • FBT Base Value vs. Amount Financed: A lease removes GST from the purchase price. Our sample vehicle costs $75,244.41, but after GST savings, the actual Amount Financed is $69,269.81. Ensure you use the ex-GST amount financed. If your car is not an EV, you will need the full drive-away price for the "FBT Base Value" field.
  • Running Costs: Look at "Package inclusions". Add up Fuel, Tyres, Servicing, Registration, and Insurance. In our sample, this totals $235.15 per cycle.
  • Admin Fee: Usually called a "Package management fee". Our sample charges $7.50 per cycle.
  • Interest Rate: Check the "Lease details" box. Our sample uses 9.28%. If your quote hides the rate, switch to "I know the quoted repayment amount" and the calculator will find the rate for you.

How to read this: this ledger is a planning tool, not tax or financial advice. It uses simplified assumptions common to retail novated lease quotes: the ATO statutory formula method for FBT; 2026–27 Australian resident income tax brackets plus the Medicare levy (assuming a single person with no dependents) and the Low Income Tax Offset; a flat nominal interest rate applied evenly across each pay period; and no allowance for HECS/HELP repayments.
